What we do at Nikitron Embedded Europe

Nikitron Embedded Europe is a small, multidisciplinary team focused on turning ideas into working embedded and IoT products. We combine electronics design, firmware, RF, and software so you don't have to coordinate multiple vendors.

On the hardware side we design schematics and PCBs, including RF and high-speed boards, with attention to layout, signal integrity and antenna integration. We also take manufacturability seriously: we review designs for DFM, select realistic components, and optimise BOMs so that your product can actually be produced and assembled at scale.

For mechanics and enclosures we offer 3D printing and rapid prototyping services, which help validate fit, ergonomics and assembly before committing to tooling. On top of the hardware we develop firmware for ARM Cortex-M and other MCUs, integrate secure connectivity, and build simple dashboards or backends so the system is usable in the real world.

If you need help with a new device, a proof-of-concept, or improving an existing product, we can often start small and iterate quickly. Our goal is to be a practical partner who understands both engineering details and real-world constraints like cost, timelines and maintenance.

Capabilities
  • Embedded MCUs: STM32, ESP32, AVR and ARM Cortex-M based controllers
  • Wireless and RF: LoRa, sub-GHz RF, BLE, Wi‑Fi and custom 2.4 GHz links (NRF24, etc.)
  • Electronics tools: KiCad, Altium-style workflows, SPICE simulation and lab measurements
  • Prototyping: in-house 3D printing, quick-turn PCBs and small batch assemblies
  • Production: design for manufacturing (DFM), test strategy and firmware update concepts (OTA, SWD, etc.)
