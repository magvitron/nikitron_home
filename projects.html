Current project

Camera Light Meter

An ESP32-C6-based instrument for measuring light while shooting.

01 / In development

A compact measurement tool for the camera bag

This project explores a dedicated light meter built around the ESP32-C6. The goal is a focused, practical device that helps photographers read the scene and make exposure decisions without reaching for a phone.

From electronics and firmware to enclosure and user experience, the project brings Nikitron's end-to-end product development approach into one small instrument.
